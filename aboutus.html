About Bada
Bada Machinery Parts Factory was established in 1989. in 30 years it has grow up from a small factory with10 workers,8 sets CNC machines. To a big modern enterprise with 8000sqm coverage and 80 workers and 40 machines The main products are types of pipes and other spare parts for tractor.
In 1991,Bada Machinery established its sales agencySitong Agricultural Mahicnery Parts Sales Agency in Baoding,Gaoyang County . The main products are Benye and Shifeng small and medium-size tractor parts.Gears,shafts,tie rod ends etc.were produced by our own factory. From that time Bada Machinery started to expand our business to most provinces in North of China.
In 1995, Fuyang Sitong Agricultural Machinery Parts Sales Agency was established to expand business in South of China,80 square meter office and 600 square meter warehouse,main products are tractor parts,engine parts and tiller parts.
In 2005,new office in Baoding was bulit,900 square meter office and 2000 square meter warehouse.

From Bada Machinery starts the export for tractor parts and meanwhile it started its experience in Exhibition abroad to extend its Export Business.
